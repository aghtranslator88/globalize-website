# Embassy Document Translation: Check the Recipient’s Requirements

**Short answer:** Translation requirements depend on the country, document, application type, and receiving authority. Use the official instructions for your case, then share them with Globalize when requesting a translation. A translation service cannot determine or guarantee an embassy decision.

## Start with the authority receiving your document

Identify the exact recipient and process before ordering a translation: for example, immigrant-visa civil documents, visitor-visa supporting documents, or legalization of an Egyptian public document for use abroad. Similar document names can follow different rules. ## What to verify before you submit

Check with the receiving authority: (1) which exact document version is required; (2) which language is accepted and whether a full translation is required; (3) whether a specific certification statement, translator qualification, stamp, signature, or format is required; (4) whether authentication or legalization is needed and in what order; and (5) whether appointment, submission, or document-validity rules apply.

Do not treat one authority’s answer as a rule for another country, visa category, document type, or institution. Official instructions can change; verify the linked page before submission. The receiving authority makes the final decision on its requirements and the application.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Globalize guarantee that an embassy will accept a translation?

No. The receiving authority decides whether the document and translation meet its requirements. Share current written instructions so the requested format can be assessed; no external outcome is guaranteed.

### Are translation rules the same for every embassy or visa type?

No. Requirements can differ by recipient, process, country, and document. The U.S. Department of State page linked above is for immigrant-visa civil documents, while GOV.UK’s guide covers visitor-visa supporting documents. Follow the official instructions for your application.

### Should I translate a document before legalization?

There is no universal sequence. Follow current instructions for the issuing country, recipient, and exact document. The linked Germany and Italy procedures illustrate why applicants should check the applicable route rather than assume one order fits every case.

### What should I send to request a translation assessment?

Send a complete, legible copy, source and target languages, recipient and purpose, requested deadline, and any written translation, certification, or formatting instructions. Do not send originals unless the relevant process requires them.
