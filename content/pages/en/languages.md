# Translation Languages and Language Pairs

**Short answer:** Arabic↔English is a core language direction at Globalize. Availability for another pair—or for a particular service, subject, format, or interpreting/localization assignment—must be checked for the specific request. A language appearing in a general list does not mean every service is available in that language.

## Core language direction

| Language direction | What this page confirms | What still needs request-specific assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic ↔ English | A core Globalize language direction for translation enquiries | The requested service, subject area, document or content type, direction, format, and any recipient-specific instruction |

This is a conservative public list of confirmed facts, not a complete inventory of every language or service Globalize may handle. No fixed language count is stated because the current approved service-by-language matrix is not available in the project record.

## Asking about another language pair

For a pair not shown here, send the source and target languages, the type of service you need, the subject or content, the approximate volume, the intended recipient or audience, and any written requirements. Globalize can assess the enquiry; this does not itself confirm availability or a delivery date.

Language availability can differ by service. Written translation, certified translation, legal or medical translation, interpretation, and localization should not be treated as interchangeable. Ask about the exact service and language direction you need.

## Choose the right service route

If your request concerns a written document, start with [Certified Translation](/en/certified) and identify the document and recipient. For spoken interpretation or website/software localization, state that explicitly in your enquiry; this directory does not confirm a language pair for either service.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which language pair is confirmed here?

Arabic↔English is confirmed as a core Globalize language direction. This does not establish availability for every service, subject, format, or deadline.

### Does a language listed by Globalize mean it is available for every service?

No. A language may be handled differently across written translation, certified work, specialist subjects, interpretation, and localization. Ask about the exact pair and service.

### Can I request a language pair not shown on this page?

Yes. Send the source and target languages and the project details for assessment. The enquiry does not guarantee availability or a delivery date.

### What details help assess a language-pair request?

Provide the service type, source and target languages, subject or content, format/platform, approximate volume, intended recipient or audience, and written requirements. Avoid sending sensitive files or access credentials in the initial enquiry.
